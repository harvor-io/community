# Harvor Code of Conduct

Harvor is committed to maintaining a respectful, welcoming, and productive open-source community.

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experiences, perspectives, and skill levels. Everyone participating in the Harvor community is expected to treat others with respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Treating others with respect and professionalism
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback
- Disagreeing with ideas without attacking the people presenting them
- Being patient with contributors of different experience levels
- Asking questions in good faith
- Helping others learn and contribute
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them
- Keeping technical discussions focused on the problem being solved

Healthy disagreement is part of building good software. Contributors are encouraged to challenge ideas, designs, and technical decisions while remaining respectful toward the people involved.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within the Harvor community:

- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately disrespectful comments
- Harassment, intimidation, or threats
- Discriminatory or demeaning language
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting productive conversations
- Repeatedly engaging in hostile or inflammatory behavior
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate sexual content
- Encouraging others to engage in unacceptable behavior
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

Criticism of code, architecture, documentation, decisions, and ideas is welcome.

Attacking or belittling the people behind them is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within Harvor community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues
- Pull requests
- GitHub Discussions
- Code reviews
- Other official Harvor communication channels

It may also apply when someone is representing Harvor in a public community or event.

## Enforcement

Harvor maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

When inappropriate behavior occurs, maintainers may take actions including:

- Asking a participant to change their behavior
- Removing or editing inappropriate content where possible
- Locking or limiting discussions
- Issuing a warning
- Temporarily restricting participation
- Permanently removing someone from Harvor community spaces

Enforcement decisions will consider the context, severity, and history of the behavior.

Our goal is not to police disagreement. Our goal is to maintain an environment where people can collaborate, disagree, learn, and build software together without being subjected to harassment or disrespect.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the Harvor maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical.

Please avoid escalating interpersonal disputes through public issues or discussions when a private report would be more appropriate.

## A Simple Rule

When in doubt:

**Be respectful. Critique ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and contribute in good faith.**

Thank you for helping make Harvor a community where people can build great software together.
